# Proportions

## Nut Mixture
| Walnuts | Pistachios (shelled, unsalted) | Sugar |    Spices<sup>1</sup>    |     Salt<sup>2</sup>    |
|:-------:|:------------------------------:|:-----:|:------------:|:-----------------------:|
|    4    |               1                |  1.5  |      *       |            *            |

## Baklava
| Filo Dough | Nut mixture<sup>3</sup> | Butter |
|:----------:|:-----------:|:------:|
|     4      |      *      |   1    |

1. Spice to taste. I used 4g of cinnamon in a nut mixture of ~325g.
2. Salt to taste. I used 2g of salt in a nut mixture of ~325g.
3. The nut mixture to dough ratio could be as low as 1:3, however, closer to 1:2 might be better. The nut mixture layers were thin in order to maintain the overall layer count.


## Syrup
| Water | Sugar | Honey | Lemon Juice |    Spice<sup>4</sup>     |
|:-----:|:-----:|:-----:|:-----------:|:------------:|
|  2.5  |   2   |  1.5  |     0.3     |      *       |

4. Spice to taste. I used 1g of cinnamon in a syrup of ~630g.

I used 1kg of Filo dough and 250g of water. The remaining proportions were worked out accordingly.

# Instructions

* Lightly grease baking dish (optionally add a bottom baking paper layer)
* Melt the butter
* Chop and mix the nut mixture ingredients in a blender.
    * My mixture was somewhat homogenous in particulate size.
* Prepare the Baklava:
    * Begin layering the Filo. Add 8 layers of dough, brushing butter thinly but completely over the each layer's surface
    * After the 8th layer is buttered, apply a thin (~0.25 cm?) layer of the nut mixture.
    * Apply 2 more Filo layers (buttering in between as before).
    * Add another nut mixture layer as before.
    * Keep repeating the 2 Filo layers to nut mixture layer steps until the nut mixture is gone. There should be about 8 layers of Filo remaining afterward.
    * Apply the remaining Filo layers the same way as the first layers.
    * Trim all excess Filo from the edges of the baking dish.
    * Slice through the dough to create pieces of the desired size.
        * I created a grid of 24 pieces, make 4 rows of 6 columns.
* Preheat oven to 180&deg;C (with fan)
* While the oven preheats, create the syrup:
    * Mix all syrup ingredients in a pot over high heat.
    * Bring the mixture to a boil
    * Reduce heat to a simmer and let thicken for 10-15 minutes
    * Turn off to let cool
* Bake the Baklava for ~30 minutes or until golden brown
* Remove the Baklava from the oven and spoon the syrup evenly over the Baklava while it's hot
